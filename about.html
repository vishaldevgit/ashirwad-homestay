About Ashirwad Homestay

A family home in the hills of Bhimtal — opened to share the peace, the mountains, and real Kumaoni hospitality.

Our Story

A Home in the Hills, Opened to Guests

Ashirwad Homestay started simply — the Pande family had two full 2-BHK sets in their Bhimtal property, and after welcoming a few guests informally, they decided to open it properly. The name "Ashirwad" — meaning divine blessing — reflects the spirit the family brings to every stay.

Ahin and Divya Pande are your hosts. They live on the premises, are available 24 hours, and genuinely enjoy hosting guests who come seeking the peace and beauty of Bhimtal — whether they're here for Kainchi Dham darshan, the 7 Taals, or simply to escape city life for a few days.

This is not a hotel managed by staff. The hosts are present, personal, and invested in making sure you have a comfortable, authentic stay.
