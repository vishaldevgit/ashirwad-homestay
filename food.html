What We Serve

Simple. Fresh. The same food we eat.

Kumaoni Specialty

Kaapa — A Dish You Won't Find in a Restaurant

Kaapa is slow-cooked spinach with black lentils — a staple Kumaoni recipe made fresh in every mountain home. Rich, earthy, and deeply satisfying with rice or roti. You won't find the real version in a tourist dhaba.

Depending on what the family is cooking that day, you may be treated to Kaapa, Bhaang ki Chutney, Gahat Dal, or other recipes unique to the Kumaon hills.
