## 📍 Location Advantages (USE THESE as selling points)

### 🚐 Kainchi Dham — KEY USP
- **Kainchi Dham Mandir** is one of the most visited spiritual sites in Uttarakhand
- Located only **20 KM from this homestay**
- VERY HIGH tourist/pilgrim traffic — people come from all over India
- Due to heavy traffic, **Government has created a Shuttle Service** from Bhimtal to Kainchi Dham Mandir
- **This homestay is only 200 METERS from the Shuttle Service Parking**
- This is a MASSIVE advantage — guests don't need personal vehicles
- Always highlight this on homepage, location page, and meta descriptions

### 🏞️ The 7 Taals (Lakes) — Unique Regional Identity
This region is famous for its chain of 7 beautiful lakes:
1. **Bhimtal** — largest lake, right in the town
2. **Sattal** — cluster of 7 interconnected lakes, forest area
3. **Naukuchiatal** — "Nine-cornered lake", deepest in region
4. **Khurpatal** — serene, less crowded, scenic
5. **Garudtal** — small, peaceful, birdwatching spot
6. **Malital** — part of Nainital, upper lake
7. **Nainital** — most famous hill station in Uttarakhand, full tourist city

> 💡 Design Idea: A visual "Lakes Map" or "7 Taals Trail" section 
> can be a unique interactive feature on the website.
