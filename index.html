Explore the Region

Base Camp for the 7 Taals

All 7 lakes of Kumaon within 22 KM. Use Ashirwad Homestay as your base — explore one lake a day.

Bhimtal Lake
Walking Distance
Largest lake in the region. Boating, island café, evening walks.
Naukuchiatal
5 KM Away
Nine-cornered lake. Deepest in the region. Peaceful & serene.
Sattal
10 KM Away
Cluster of 7 interconnected lakes. Dense forest. Birdwatching.
Khurpatal
12 KM Away
Less crowded, scenic, emerald-green water. Perfect for quiet visits.
Garudtal
~15 KM Away
Small & peaceful. Great birdwatching spot for nature lovers.
Malital
~20 KM Away
Upper lake of Nainital. The quiet, less-visited end of Nainital Lake.
Nainital
22 KM Away
Uttarakhand's most famous hill station. Full day trip from Bhimtal.
